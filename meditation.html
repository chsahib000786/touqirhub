A gentle daily rhythm

You don't need all of this every day — even one small piece helps.

Morning

  • A few slow breaths before you check your phone
  • Set one simple intention for the day

Midday

  • A short pause between tasks
  • Notice your shoulders, jaw, and breath

Evening

  • Let the day's pace slow down
  • A body scan before sleep

A gentle note. These practices are meant for general relaxation and everyday stress — they are not therapy and not a substitute for professional mental health care.
