Age Calculator

Calculate your exact age from your date of birth. Get your age in years, months and days, plus additional time information.

Enter your date of birth to calculate your age.

How to Use the Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth and select Calculate Age. The calculator uses today's date to calculate your current age in years, months and days.

What Does the Age Calculator Show?

The calculator provides your current age and additional information such as the approximate total number of months, weeks and days since your date of birth. It also calculates the date of your next birthday.

Age Calculator Example

If your date of birth is January 15, 2000, the calculator compares that date with today's date and calculates your exact age based on the difference between the two dates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my age calculated?

Your age is calculated by comparing your date of birth with the current date and accounting for completed years, months and days.

Can I calculate my exact age in days?

Yes. The calculator also provides an approximate total number of days based on the dates entered.

Can I use this calculator on a phone?

Yes. The calculator is designed to work on both mobile devices and desktop browsers.
